Beef and Shells

A one-pot dish of beef and shell pasta

pasta and beef, in a creamy, beefy tomato and extra-sharp Cheddar sauce

You'll turn a humble pound of ground beef into a family-friendly dinner that is all homemade.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces medium pasta shells
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1/2 medium white onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 (8 ounce) cans tomato sauce
  • 3/4 cup half-and-half
  • 2 cups shredded extra-sharp Cheddar cheese
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook for 8 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add ground beef and break it up with the back of a spoon while it browns.
  3. Add onions to the pot and cook, stirring, until they begin to soften, about 3 minutes.
  4. Mix in garlic and Italian seasoning. Cook and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Sprinkle flour on top and stir. The mixture will be dry.
  6. Slowly whisk in beef broth, then add tomato sauce. Whisk until smooth. Taste and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Bring sauce to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer, stirring frequently, until reduced and slightly thickened.
  8. Add beef, pasta shells, and half-and-half to sauce and mix until well combined.
  9. Stir in cheese until completely melted.
  10. Serve and enjoy
